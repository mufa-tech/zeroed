Built for the line, not the lab

Fully offline

Works at the range, in the field and in airplane mode. No servers, nothing to sign in to.

Big-number HUD

One screen, one hold. Readable in direct sun with the black/white Sun theme, and at night with the red theme.

Real solver

Point-mass RK4 with standard G1/G7 drag tables, multi-BC, segmented wind, incline and full atmosphere.
