Support

Quick answers. Still stuck? Email us and a real person replies.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Settings → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought Pro with. Restoring needs an internet connection for a moment; after that, Pro works offline.

How does the 7-day trial work?

You can try every Pro feature for 7 days. Nothing renews and nothing is charged automatically. When the trial ends, the app goes back to the free version unless you buy Pro.

How do I switch units?

Settings → Units: metric or imperial for distance, speed, temperature and pressure. Each scope profile has its own adjustment unit (MRAD or MOA) and click value.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Settings → Backup → Export creates one file with all your profiles. Save it to Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or anywhere you like. On the new phone, open Settings → Backup → Import and pick that file. Restore your Pro purchase separately.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Everything is calculated on your phone. Only buying or restoring Pro needs a connection.

How accurate is it?

Zeroed uses a point-mass RK4 solver with standard G1/G7 drag tables. Results are only as good as your inputs: measure MV with a chronograph, use the bullet maker’s BC and enter real conditions. Then true the solution with real drop at distance.

Is the barometer reading station pressure?

Yes. The phone sensor gives station pressure, which is what the solver wants. If you enter pressure by hand, use station pressure, not the sea-level value from a weather report.
