Strelok Pro alternative: offline, one-time purchase

If your ballistic data lived in the original Strelok Pro, here is what changed, how to move your rifle and load into Zeroed field by field, and what Zeroed does not do yet.

Facts on this page checked on 2026-10-20. Store pages and prices change, so follow the source links.

What happened

  • The original Strelok Pro by Igor Borisov (Android package com.borisov.strelokpro) is no longer on Google Play. Its store page returns “not found”. Source: Google Play page, checked on 2026-10-20.
  • “Strelok Pro – Ballistic Platform” is a separate app from a different publisher: Strelok LLC on the App Store and Kamil Suleymanov on Google Play (package com.strelok.ballistic). Sources: App Store (US), Google Play, checked on 2026-10-20.
  • That app is free to download, with subscriptions and a lifetime purchase. Its store listing describes GPT-based and live-weather features, which need a network connection.

If the copy you already have still works for you, there is no need to rush. This guide is for anyone setting up a new phone, or anyone who wants an offline calculator with a one-time price.

Before you switch: write your numbers down

Zeroed can’t read files from other apps, so you copy the numbers by hand. It takes about ten minutes per rifle. Field names differ between apps, so the quickest way is a screenshot of each screen in your old app. You need:

  • Rifle: sight height, zero range, barrel twist rate and direction, scope click value.
  • Load: bullet weight, caliber, bullet length (if you have it), BC and its drag model (G1 or G7), any velocity-banded BCs, muzzle velocity and the temperature it was measured at.
  • Anything you trued: the trued muzzle velocity or BC, and the range where you confirmed it.

Conditions: Solve tab

  • Temperature, Humidity, and Pressure from the phone’s barometer (station pressure) or from Altitude. Station pressure is the real pressure where you stand, not the sea-level number in a weather report.
  • Wind speed and Direction as a clock position: 3 o’clock is wind from the right. There is a wind bracket and segmented wind.
  • Incline: + uphill, − downhill.
  • Coriolis (Pro): Latitude and Shot direction (compass bearing to the target), typed in by hand. No location permission.

In Settings, set Units (Metric or Imperial) and Show corrections in (MRAD, MOA or clicks) to match your turrets.

Check it, then true it

  1. Compare. With identical inputs, put 300, 600 and 800 m side by side in both apps. Small differences are normal: solvers differ in drag-table interpolation and air-density details. A big difference almost always means a units mix-up, a wrong zero range, the wrong sight height, or G1 and G7 swapped.
  2. Confirm with real shots. A trued value from another app was fitted to that app’s solver. Start with it, then confirm at distance again.
  3. True it in Zeroed (Pro): True this load, then enter the hold that actually hit. Zeroed adjusts velocity or BC to fit.
  4. Print the DOPE card as an image or PDF. Free cards carry a watermark.

What Zeroed does

  • 100% offline, even in airplane mode. No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Point-mass RK4 solver, G1/G7 BC and multi-BC. It agrees with an independent open-source solver within 0.33% (worst case) across 7 cartridges and 42 ranges.
  • Wind bracket, segmented wind, incline, phone barometer.
  • DOPE chart shared as an image or a PDF card, to 800 m / 875 yd free and 2000 m / 2200 yd with Pro.
  • Pro: spin drift, Coriolis, truing, unlimited rifles and loads, the bullet library, the Night theme, and the Lab (chronograph CSV import from Garmin Xero or LabRadar, group size from a photo, load development log, hunt planner, PRS stage card).
  • English, Deutsch, Türkçe, Русский.

What Zeroed doesn’t do (yet)

  • No Bluetooth. No Kestrel and no rangefinder connection. Chronograph data comes in as a CSV file or typed in.
  • No import from other apps. You copy your numbers over, as above.
  • No load data, ever. The load log only records your own tests.
  • No watch app and no iPhone version yet.

Free is a complete calculator and stays that way: 1 rifle, 2 loads, out to 800 m / 875 yd, factory loads, the DOPE table and a watermarked card. Your data stays on your phone. Back it up with Settings → Back up to a file, and bring it back with Restore from a file.
